# Design direction

An architectural workbench: cool white #F7F9FB, graphite #202831, blue slate #47637A, mist #DEE6ED, spruce #446657. Georgia display lettering contrasts with an understated system sans interface. A large interactive axonometric home is the entry point. The left project rail contains the actual workflow, while the right is the review canvas. Listing intake, photo evidence and editable scene review share one workspace. No marketing metric cards or generated customer imagery.
